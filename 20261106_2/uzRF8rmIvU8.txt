You need to find out who the money mule has been talking to.and then once you find out who the person is, and then you tracing back again to see who exactly are they working for. The attack on First Bank's ATM systems back in twenty sixteen was in a way a classic of that kind of cyber crime genre, a mass scale attack.On one country, one bank systems, with the aim of extricating as much cash from the ATM network as the criminals could, in as short a time as possible. It came during a period where this was rife. There were gangs around the world doing all of these kind of attacks, and they were called in the criminal community, jackpotting, because the experience of making a ATM spew out money at you is a bit like winning the jackpot at a casino.
